The four digits of a NACA airfoil code describe its shape: the first digit is the maximum camber as a percentage of chord length, the second is the position of that camber along the chord (in tenths), and the last two are the thickness as a percentage of chord. Dragging the sliders edits the code live and redraws the cross-section.

The angle of attack is the tilt between the airfoil's chord line and the oncoming airflow. The animated lines are an illustration of how flow bends around the shape as that angle changes — not a physical simulation.
